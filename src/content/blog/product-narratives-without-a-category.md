Category creation is usually described as an act of imagination. In practice it is an act of translation.

## Start from a known shape

Buyers reason by analogy. Anchor to a familiar category first, then name the specific way your product breaks from it. "Like X, except the Y assumption no longer holds" gets further than a brand-new noun.

## Name the old workflow before naming the new one

If people cannot articulate what they do today, they cannot feel the cost of it. Describe the current workaround in enough detail that the reader recognizes their own week in it.

## Earn the new noun

A category name is the last artifact you produce, not the first. It should compress a story the market already half-believes.
