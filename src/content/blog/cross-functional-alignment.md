The gap between product and marketing is rarely about goodwill. It is about cadence: sprints move in two-week increments, campaigns move in quarters.

## Translate at the milestone, not the release

Pick the handful of sprint milestones that change what a customer can do, and treat those as the marketing beats. Everything else is a changelog entry.

## One shared artifact

A single living document that names the audience, the claim, and the proof for each upcoming beat removes most of the recurring meetings.

## Make the feedback loop visible

Send win/loss themes back to product on the same cadence product sends roadmap updates to marketing. Alignment is a two-way trade.
