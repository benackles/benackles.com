Product marketing gets measured on the metrics that are easiest to collect, which are rarely the metrics that matter.

## Adoption depth beats adoption breadth

The number of accounts that touched a feature once is a vanity number. The number that made it part of a weekly workflow is the one that predicts renewal.

## Watch sales velocity, not just pipeline

Better messaging shows up as shorter cycles and fewer competitive bake-offs before it shows up as more leads.

## Instrument the story

Tag the narrative, not only the campaign. If you cannot tell which positioning a closed-won deal heard, you cannot learn from it.
