Feature grids are comfortable. They are also the fastest way to be evaluated on someone else's terms.

## Executives do not buy features

They buy a reduction in risk, a change in cost structure, or a new capability the business did not previously have. Every feature claim should ladder to one of those three.

## Translate the grid

Take your top five differentiators and write the business consequence of each in one sentence, with no product nouns in it. If you cannot, the differentiator is not yet an enterprise argument.

## Give the champion a document they can forward

The person who loves your product is rarely the person who signs. Your job is to arm the champion with something that survives being forwarded without you in the room.
