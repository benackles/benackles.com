A tier 1 launch is not a bigger tier 2 launch. It is a different instrument entirely: more players, a longer rehearsal, and far less room for a missed cue.

## Score the whole thing before the first rehearsal

Every function needs to know what it owns and when it plays. Product owns readiness criteria, engineering owns the flag and the rollback, sales owns the pipeline motion, and marketing owns the story that holds it together.

## Rehearse the story, not just the timeline

Launch timelines get reviewed obsessively. The narrative rarely does. Run the positioning past the people who will have to repeat it under pressure — an AE on a discovery call, a support rep on a ticket — well before launch week.

## Measure the second week, not the first

Launch-day traffic tells you how good your distribution was. Week-two adoption tells you whether the story matched the product.
